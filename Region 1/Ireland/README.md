# Ireland / Éire (EI)

Amateur Radio in Ireland has been active since 1932.

The regulator in Ireland the Commission for Communications Regulation ([ComReg](https://www.comreg.ie)). 
It controls license approvals and callsign allocations.

The hobby is represented to ComReg by individual submissions and parent organisations like the Irish Radio Transmitters Society ([IRTS](https://www.irts.ie/)).

Clubs are often affiliated with the representative body and share resources such as insurance and events.

To be eligible to apply for a licence applicants are required to have successfully passed a CEPT/HAREC TR/61-02 level examination. The applicants are assigned a CEPT Class 1 licence (if they pass a Morse Test at 5 wpm), or CEPT Class 2 licence (if they have not passed the Morse Test).


# Commission for Communications Regulation (ComReg) information

The regulator controls the radio spectrum on behalf of the Government and in doing so also manages license allocation and certification. Radio amateurs are expected to be qualified and hold a current amateur callsign.

* [Amateur Radio homepage](https://www.comreg.ie/industry/radio-spectrum/licensing/search-licence-type/radio-amateurs-2/)
* [Amateur Station Licence Guidelines](https://www.comreg.ie/media/2023/05/ComReg-0945R6.pdf)


# Irish Radio Transmitters Society (IRTS) information

The Irish Radio Transmitters Society ([IRTS](https://www.irts.ie/)) is one representative to the regulator.
